Packaging quality-control planning

Agree what will be checked before the production run starts.

Start with your product

Create a specification covering structure, material, artwork version, quantity, features, reference samples and packing. Identify which cosmetic and functional points matter most to the product.

Review the options

Plan appropriate checks for dimensions, print, finish, seals, assembly or fitted contents. Define methods and acceptance criteria rather than relying on general quality language.

Confirm before approval

Keep inspection findings tied to the order and specification. Discuss any nonconformity, correction and approval before release; no universal performance rating is implied.
