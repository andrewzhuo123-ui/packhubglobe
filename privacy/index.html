Privacy information

Privacy information.

Understand what this website does with the information you choose to share.

Your enquiry

Submitting the packaging form sends your details to our website server for email delivery to andrew@packhubglobe.com. A success message means the server accepted the enquiry for email delivery; it does not confirm inbox receipt. The download option creates a text brief locally in your browser. You can attach up to 5 artwork, image or reference files (5 MB per file, 10 MB total). Uploaded files are temporarily processed on the server and included as email attachments; the website does not publish them or keep a separate permanent upload copy.

Information you choose to provide

An email enquiry may include your name, business email, company, product requirements and files you attach. Provide only information needed to discuss the packaging project. Avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive data.

Purpose and contact

We use enquiry information to discuss specifications, provide quotations, arrange samples and handle the requested business relationship. Email and hosting providers may process information as part of delivering those services. Hosting may also record ordinary technical access logs.

Your choices

You can contact andrew@packhubglobe.com to ask about your enquiry information or request correction or deletion, subject to any applicable record-keeping requirements. Form input is not intentionally saved to browser storage by this website.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp links open an external chat service. Information you choose to share there is handled through WhatsApp and its own privacy terms. A link does not send a message automatically.

Website resources

Images, fonts, styles and scripts are hosted with this website. The site prepares event names and page paths in browser memory for a future analytics integration. No external analytics collector, advertising pixel or marketing-cookie system is connected. Enquiry text, contact details and file contents are not included in these events. External reference links have their own policies. The companies behind the brand are identified on the About page.
