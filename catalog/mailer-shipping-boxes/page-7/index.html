Mailer & shipping boxes.

Compare mailer and shipping-box references around the packed contents and delivery route. Review internal dimensions, board, closing method and any insert or protective layer. Printed branding and opening features should work with the parcel arrangement. Agree the relevant sample and handling checks for the complete pack. Page 7.
