Fragile snacks, fine powders and dense ingredients occupy different volumes at the same weight. A filled trial helps establish the usable size before print is approved.

Check what matters.

Food-contact and shelf-life claims require evidence for the selected material and intended use. Review sealing, distribution and applicable labelling with qualified advisers.

What to include in your enquiry

Product texture, oil content, moisture sensitivity, fill weight, intended shelf life and packing process.

Include the quantity for each component and design, artwork status, delivery country and target arrival window.
