Magnetic boxes.

Review magnetic presentation-box references as a board, wrap, lid and insert system. Confirm the closing arrangement and whether the box is supplied assembled or flat. Check loaded lid alignment, product access and print across the opening sequence. The final magnetic configuration belongs on the approved construction drawing. Page 3.
