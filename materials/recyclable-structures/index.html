Recyclable packaging structures

Evaluate the complete structure against the destination’s collection and processing route.

Start with the requirement

Paper, film, labels, closures, coatings, inserts and adhesives can create different end-of-life questions within one package. A mono-material direction may simplify a brief, but does not prove local collection or sorting access.

Connect the material and the process

Compare material reduction, protection and the applicable recovery system together. A pack that loses product or fails in transit creates a different tradeoff from a well-matched package.

Agree the evidence and approval

Request evidence for the exact claim, material structure and market. Avoid broad labels such as universally recyclable or biodegradable without qualified evidence and any required conditions.

Prepare a useful project brief.

Include the product, packaging format, approximate size or fill weight, quantity per design, artwork and destination. Mark requirements that are fixed and decisions that need review.

Request a clear quotation covering the selected specification, sample route, production basis and shipping assumptions. Prepare your packaging brief.
