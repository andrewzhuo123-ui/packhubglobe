Materials & finishes

Packaging printing & finishes

Use print and finishing to support the graphic design and the structure.

Start with your product

Full-color print, spot colors, white ink, foil, embossing and selected coatings produce different visual and tactile effects. Availability depends on material, production route, artwork and quantity.

Review the options

Define solid areas, small text, color priorities and fold placement. A bright pouch, a coated carton and an uncoated card may need coordinated references rather than one identical ink instruction.

Confirm before approval

Approve a suitable proof or physical sample. Check registration, foil or coating placement, folds, scuffing and readability on the final material. An illustrative render is a design direction, not print approval.
