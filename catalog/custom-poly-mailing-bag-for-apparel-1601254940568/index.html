Define the complete shipping envelope

A poly mailer is an outer parcel layer for a specified product and packing arrangement. Measure the folded goods, retail pack and any protective layer before setting the finished width and length. Include working space for the closing flap and planned delivery label. Confirm the usable opening and insertion method on a sample. The appearance of a branded mailing envelope does not establish its film thickness, puncture performance or protection for a different packed product.

Review film, seams and adhesive flap

Specify opacity, film construction, thickness convention and seam arrangement together with the closing strip. If a return closure or opening strip is requested, show it on the drawing. Try the actual packed corners against the film and review the flap after sealing. Avoid treating a general material name as a performance result. Keep any material or environmental statement tied to documents for the agreed construction rather than to claims printed on the reference image.

Plan print around labels and opening

Reserve space for address labels, carrier marks, the closing flap and any opening strip. Confirm the artwork orientation after the envelope is packed and folded. Review contrast and opacity where the goods could show through the film. Keep important brand and product information clear of seams and areas that will be covered during shipment. Inspect a formed, labelled sample using the actual packing method before approving print placement and finishing.

Test packing and delivery handling

Use representative goods to trial insertion, closure and customer opening. Check film contact with corners, seam loading and whether the closing strip remains accessible to the packing team. Agree the relevant delivery or handling checks for the complete parcel rather than for an empty bag. Document the material, dimensions, closure and contents used in the trial. Revisit those checks when a product, protective layer or bag construction changes in a later order.
