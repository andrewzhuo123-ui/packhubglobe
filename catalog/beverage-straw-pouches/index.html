Beverage & straw pouches.

Review beverage-pouch references showing straw access and top finger openings. Confirm the insertion point, top sealing arrangement, any reclosure and the filling route separately. This opening style does not establish a screw-cap spout. Trial the intended beverage, straw and carrying method with a matching filled sample.
