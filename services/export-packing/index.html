Export packing & shipment planning

Include the outer shipment configuration in the packaging brief.

Start with the requirement

Provide the destination, expected mode of transport, unloading conditions and delivery window. Distinguish product production timing from freight and customs-related steps.

Connect the material and the process

Review packing quantities, carton dimensions, weight, protection between finished surfaces and pallet direction where applicable. Flat supply and assembled supply may require different shipment plans.

Agree the evidence and approval

Confirm shipping terms, marks, documents, responsibility and arrival assumptions in the quotation. Prices and delivery dates require project-specific confirmation.

Prepare a useful project brief.

Include the product, packaging format, approximate size or fill weight, quantity per design, artwork and destination. Mark requirements that are fixed and decisions that need review.

Request a clear quotation covering the selected specification, sample route, production basis and shipping assumptions. Prepare your packaging brief.
