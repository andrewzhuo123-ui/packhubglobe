Privacy information

A clear starting point
for your enquiry.

Understand what this website does with the information you choose to share.

Your enquiry

The packaging form prepares an email draft or a downloadable text brief in your browser. It does not automatically submit your details to a form server. Opening the draft passes the details to your chosen email application; sending that email delivers it to andrew@packhubglobe.com.

Information you choose to provide

An email enquiry may include your name, business email, company, product requirements and files you attach. Provide only information needed to discuss the packaging project. Avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive data.

Purpose and contact

We use enquiry information to discuss specifications, provide quotations, arrange samples and handle the requested business relationship. Email and hosting providers may process information as part of delivering those services. Hosting may also record ordinary technical access logs.

Your choices

You can contact andrew@packhubglobe.com to ask about your enquiry information or request correction or deletion, subject to any applicable record-keeping requirements. Form input is not intentionally saved to browser storage by this website.

Website resources

Images, fonts, styles and scripts are hosted with this website. No visitor analytics, advertising pixels or marketing-cookie system is installed in this version. External reference links have their own policies. The companies behind the brand are identified on the About page.
