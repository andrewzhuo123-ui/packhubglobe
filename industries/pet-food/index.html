Pet food & treat packaging

Build your packaging range.

A pouch that fits a dense kibble may not fit a light treat at the same weight. Compare filled dimensions, seals and the secondary-carton layout before choosing artwork proportions.

Check what matters.

Review puncture risks, seal contamination and moisture or aroma requirements. Do not assume a bag is suitable because another pet product uses a similar shape.

What to include in your enquiry

Product edges, density, oil content, fill weight, closure needs and the expected storage conditions.

Include the quantity for each component and design, artwork status, delivery country and target arrival window.
