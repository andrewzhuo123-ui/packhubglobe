Packaging sleeves & wraps.

Compare printed sleeves and wraps around the underlying product, tray or plain package. Confirm wrap length, face panels, folds, overlap and closing method on the actual object. Review information visibility and fitting before approving print. A sleeve is a separate printed component rather than a complete shipping box.
