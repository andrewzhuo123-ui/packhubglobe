Kraft shipping boxes.

Review kraft and corrugated mailing references with the product they will carry. Confirm the actual liner, board construction and printed surface separately from the image's color or finish. Compare dimensions, closure and packing clearance on a formed sample; a kraft appearance alone does not establish parcel protection.
