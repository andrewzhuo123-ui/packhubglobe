Multi-product packaging sourcing

Coordinate different packaging components in one clear procurement brief.

Start with your product

List each box, bag, pouch, card and label with its quantity per size and design. Identify common colors, artwork versions, packing relationships and the launch window.

Review the options

Confirm how the components fit together. A card, seal and outer bag can look coordinated yet fail if their sizes, finishes or adhesive needs were specified separately.

Confirm before approval

Maintain a component list and approved references. Quote each product line clearly, including responsible supply, samples, commercial terms and the planned delivery sequence.
