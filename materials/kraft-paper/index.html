Kraft paper packaging

Review paper appearance and function as separate decisions.

Start with your product

Kraft paper gives bags, wraps and some packaging surfaces a characteristic visual direction. Natural shade can vary, and printed colors may differ from artwork viewed against a white screen.

Review the options

Define paper, print coverage, handling and the required package structure. Review light inks, dark solids and fine text on the chosen paper rather than approving from a generic white proof.

Confirm before approval

Ask about the finished construction and any coating, adhesive or liner. Natural appearance is not evidence that a complete package is recyclable, compostable or suitable for direct food contact.
