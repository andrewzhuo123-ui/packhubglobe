A closer look at custom cardboard display

PHG-1601424233270 is the product reference to include when you contact PackHubGlobe. The captured material description is Recycle Paperboard; the listed size direction is Custom Size Accepted. Printing information for this reference includes Offset Printing, Glossy Lamination, UV Coating, Embossing. Use these details to start a specification, then identify any changes needed for your product. The image shows a packaging design direction rather than a fixed stock item, and the finished order should be defined by its agreed artwork, construction and sample. Custom orders start at 500 pieces.

Design the display around replenishment

A cardboard display must work for the product, retail location and the person who replenishes it. Provide product dimensions, weight, quantity per shelf and the required footprint. Decide whether the display ships filled or is assembled in store. Review viewing height, access and how the last few products remain visible. A promotional image cannot establish shelf load or stability, so treat the loaded structure as a separate design and sample decision.

Structure and support

Discuss the base, shelves, dividers, header and any internal support as one assembly. Concentrated product weight can stress a shelf differently from an evenly distributed load. Consider the floor or counter surface and the way the unit is moved. Agree assembly steps and the parts that need clear identification. Material and construction should be reviewed with the planned load and use period; a generic board description does not establish a display capacity.

Graphics for the retail setting

Separate the headline message from product information and assembly instructions. A shopper should understand the category and offer from the intended viewing distance, while staff need practical loading and replenishment guidance. Keep critical copy away from folds, slots and hidden joins. Review the header with the assembled display and stocked products in place. Coordinated colors on shelves and side panels can support recognition without making every surface equally busy.

Loaded sample review

Assemble the sample using the planned instructions and load it with representative products. Check shelf fit, access, stability, replenishment and the final footprint. If the display ships filled, review the outer shipping arrangement and protection as well. Record the construction, product load and conditions used in the evaluation. Changes to the product set, shelf quantity or structural parts should trigger an appropriate review before the display is repeated.

Customize the package as a complete system

Bring the contents, package and printed information into one brief. Tell us which reference features you want to retain and which you want to change: dimensions, material, closure, insert, color, printed panels or finishing. Supply a logo and describe the artwork status, even if the design is still being developed. Structural decisions should be resolved before important text is positioned on a final dieline. If several components need to coordinate, list their individual quantities and explain how they fit together at packing and delivery.

Plan the sample and quality checks

Choose the sample according to the decisions still open. A structural sample helps review size, assembly and product access. A printed reference helps review artwork and selected appearance questions; confirm how closely its material and print route represent the order. Agree the important cosmetic and functional checks before production. Inspect the package with its intended contents and record the approved revision. A change to the product, material, closure or insert may require the relevant decision to be reviewed again rather than carried over automatically.

MOQ, quotation and delivery planning

The minimum order quantity for this catalog reference is 500 pieces. State the quantity for each size and printed design so the quotation can identify the proposed production split. Ask for tooling or setup, sampling, product supply, export packing and freight to be explained where applicable. Production scheduling follows the agreed specification and approvals; delivery also depends on the destination and shipping arrangement. Include your required arrival window in the first message, and keep any change to artwork, quantity or packing visible when comparing a revised quotation.

What should I send for an accurate quote?
