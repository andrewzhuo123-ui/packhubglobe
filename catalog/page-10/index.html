Packaging selection & buyer’s decision guide

Choose a structure around your product, packing process and delivery route. These catalog photos are design references, not fixed stock specifications. Custom catalog projects start at 500 pieces; material, dimensions, printing, samples, price and delivery are confirmed for each project.

Compare packaging structures

StructureTypical applicationWhat to confirm
Corrugated mailerEcommerce parcels and subscription kitsReview flute construction, packed weight, internal clearance and transit protection. A board weight alone does not specify compression strength.
Folding cartonRetail bottles, jars and small packaged goodsReview board grade, caliper, grain direction, closure and any insert. Approve product fit and assembly on a structural sample.
Rigid gift boxPresentation sets, jewelry and giftingReview wrapped board, lid fit, hinges or magnets, inserts and assembly. Compare assembled shipping volume with collapsible options.
Flexible pouchFood, coffee, dry goods and refill applicationsStart with contents, filling process and required barrier. Confirm the full laminate, thickness, seal, closure and any food-contact documentation.

For a coordinated range, compare the outer box, protective insert, carry bag and labels together. Measure the actual product, allow for intended protection and handling, and identify the quantity per size and design. Confirm the assembled pack before scaling production. Use the quotation checklist to prepare the brief.

Compare print and surface finishes

FinishVisual or tactile purposeSample and specification checks
Matte or gloss laminationA film layer changes surface appearance and feel.Review scuffing, fold performance, film identity and local recycling compatibility; a matte finish alone does not establish scratch resistance.
Hot foilMetallic detail for logos and selected artwork.Confirm foil type, line width, registration and adhesion on the selected substrate. Ask for a representative finish sample.
Spot UVGloss contrast on selected artwork areas.Confirm coating type, coverage, registration and curing. Avoid assuming all UV coatings share the same environmental or food-contact status.
Embossing or debossingRaised or recessed detail in the substrate.Review relief depth, paper grain, board thickness and reverse-side appearance. Fine details and coated materials need a physical proof.

Choose finishes after reviewing artwork and the substrate. A decorative effect does not establish moisture protection, transit performance or sustainability. Where appearance matters, compare a physical print or finish sample under the agreed lighting and approve the final artwork separately from structural fit. Explore printing and finishes.

Material and approval checklist

For paper packaging, specify the paper or board grade, thickness, construction and intended load. For flexible packaging, specify the contents, fill weight or volume, filling temperature, barrier needs and sealing process. Avoid substituting a generic material name for a full construction. Request documentation relevant to the destination market and actual intended use.

A blank structural sample checks shape, assembly and fit. A printed sample or proof addresses artwork and color, while a production sample can address the agreed materials and finishing. Identify which decisions each sample must resolve. Confirm the approved drawing, artwork version, material specification, quantity and packing arrangement in writing. Review sampling options.

Frequently asked B2B ordering questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom packaging?

Custom catalog products start at 500 pieces. Specify the quantity for each size and printed design; confirm the production split in the written quotation. Selecting several references does not combine their minimum quantities.

Can I request boxes, bags and labels in one enquiry?

Yes. Add references to the quote basket, review the selection and continue to the enquiry form. Include the dimensions, quantity and artwork versions for each item in your project requirements. The selected reference numbers travel with the same enquiry and downloaded brief.

How long do samples and production take?

Sampling and production schedules depend on structure, materials, print, finish and approval requirements. Share the required arrival date and delivery country. Confirm sampling, artwork approval, production and transit milestones separately before placing the order.

Which artwork and dieline files should I prepare?

Share available vector artwork, logos and a reference image through the enquiry form. Confirm the structural drawing, dimensions, bleed, safe areas, cut lines and separate finish layers before production. Request the correct dieline for the agreed structure; sample costs and artwork services are confirmed with the project.

Are the materials certified or recyclable?

A catalog image or material name is not certification evidence. State the destination market, intended use and required documentation. Confirm certificates and test reports applicable to the actual material and finished construction. Coatings, laminates, adhesives, inserts and local collection systems affect recycling options. Supplied certificates and a paperboard sample report can be reviewed on the Factory page; match their scope to your order.

How should I specify shipping and delivery requirements?

Provide the delivery country, postcode, packed product weight and target arrival window. Ask for the shipping method, packing arrangement, applicable trade terms and any duties or taxes to be stated in the quotation. Transit and customs clearance are separate from production time.
