Packaging services

Packaging artwork preparation

Prepare artwork that can be checked against the agreed dieline and print route.

Start with your product

Provide the current dieline, final copy, artwork files, color references and intended finishes. Keep cut, fold, glue, seal and non-print zones identifiable to the prepress team.

Review the options

Check bleed, image resolution, fonts, line detail, small type, white ink and finish layers where applicable. Review critical information at actual size, including barcodes and QR codes.

Confirm before approval

Approve an identified artwork version and proof. The product owner remains responsible for wording, claims, translations and correct codes; preflight does not establish regulatory approval.
