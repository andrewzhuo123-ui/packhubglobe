Frequently Asked Questions

What information do I need for a quotation?

Start with the packaging type and your project requirements. Quantity per design, dimensions with units, artwork status and destination help us prepare a more precise quotation. Approximate details are welcome.

Can I customize the packaging size?

Yes. Share your product measurements and explain how it will be packed, displayed or shipped. Confirm internal versus external dimensions and review the fit with a structural sample.

What materials are available?

Compare paperboard, corrugated board, rigid-box wraps, kraft paper, films and label stocks. The final structure depends on your product and use. Explore materials and finishes.

Can I request samples?

Tell us what you need to check: structure, product fit, print or a finish. Sample availability, charges and timing are confirmed for your project. Review sample options.

What artwork files do you accept?

Attach PDF or AI artwork, or JPG and PNG reference images, to your enquiry. Confirm the dieline, bleed, fonts, image resolution and color references before print approval. See artwork preparation.

How does the production process work?

Share your brief, review the specification and quotation, then approve the agreed sample and artwork. Production, quality checks, export packing and delivery follow the confirmed order. View production capabilities.
