Paper boxes.

Compare folding cartons, corrugated mailers, presentation boxes and carry boxes. Begin with the complete contents, opening routine and packing method, then review board, dimensions and any insert. A printed exterior does not establish transit performance. Confirm the assembled sample and the final construction for your order. Page 35.
