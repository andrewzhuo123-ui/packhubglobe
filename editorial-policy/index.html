Editorial & image policy.

How we present packaging guidance, design references and project information.

Purpose

The packaging notebook helps buyers ask clearer questions and prepare comparable specifications. Product, material and process guidance is framed around the actual application rather than a universal promise.

Claims and evidence

We do not publish invented prices, delivery guarantees, ratings, test results or certifications. Numerical performance and compliance claims need evidence for the relevant material, structure, intended use and conditions.

Imagery

Original fictional logos, slogans and photorealistic design references demonstrate graphic directions. They are labelled as concepts and are not used as proof of a factory, certificate or customer relationship.

Sources and revisions

Company information is linked to source profiles on the About and Factory pages. Guidance should be revised when a relevant specification or source changes. We do not change dates solely to imply freshness.

Corrections

Email andrew@packhubglobe.com with the page URL and the point that needs review. For a purchasing decision, request a written specification and suitable sample or evidence for your actual project.
