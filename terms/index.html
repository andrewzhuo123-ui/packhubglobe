Quotation information

Quotation & website terms.

The points below explain how website information relates to a packaging enquiry and an agreed order.

Information and quotations

This website introduces packaging formats and decisions to review. A product page or design reference is not a binding quotation. Price, minimum quantity, materials, features, samples, production, payment and delivery are confirmed in the written quotation and agreed order.

Specifications and artwork

Confirm dimensions, material, print, finish, quantity and artwork versions before production. The customer should approve product wording, claims, translations, barcodes and other content, and have the right to use any supplied artwork.

Samples and timing

Samples answer the decisions agreed for their sample type. A sample or illustration does not establish every aspect of final performance. Production and shipping dates require project-specific confirmation; delivery assumptions should be clear in the quotation.

Design references

Original virtual-brand images on this website illustrate printing and packaging directions. They do not represent endorsements, registered customer brands, test results or completed client orders. Requested finishes and features are subject to material and production review.

Responsible supply

The written quotation identifies the supplying company and the agreed commercial terms. Read About and Factory for the companies and areas of responsibility behind PackHubGlobe. Contact andrew@packhubglobe.com for a project-specific clarification.
