Folding cartons.

Compare paperboard cartons around product fit, panel layout and the actual flap or base construction. Confirm usable dimensions, board, grain, scoring and closing method on a formed sample. Review print and finishing on the selected surface. A gift theme or cosmetic illustration does not turn a folding carton into a rigid presentation box.
